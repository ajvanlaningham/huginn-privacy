Privacy Policy

Huginn — a self-hosted, single-user personal AI assistant

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Overview and Purpose

Huginn is a self-hosted personal AI assistant operated by a single user on private homelab infrastructure. This privacy policy explains how Huginn handles information when it connects to third-party services (such as Google) on behalf of its sole operator.

Huginn is not a multi-tenant service. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts for anyone other than its owner.

Scope

This policy applies to the Huginn instance operated by the owner identified in the Contact section below. It covers OAuth tokens and any data retrieved from authorized third-party APIs for the purpose of running personal automations.

Information We Collect

Huginn only handles data that is necessary for its owner's personal automations:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by third-party providers (e.g. Google) after the owner grants consent.
  • Google Calendar data, such as event titles, times, locations and descriptions, and the list of the owner's calendars. Huginn requests the calendar.readonly, calendar.events and calendar.calendarlist.readonly scopes.
  • Gmail data, such as message contents and metadata, only once the owner enables it. Gmail access is planned and not enabled as of the effective date above. This policy will list its scopes before it is.
  • Conversations and assistant memory, including notes Huginn keeps from the owner's requests, which may draw on the data above.

Huginn collects nothing directly from anyone but its owner. However, calendar events and email can contain information about other people, such as names, email addresses and the contents of their messages. Huginn handles that information only as part of the owner's own account data. No visitor to this privacy policy page is tracked, profiled, or asked to provide personal information.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to carry out the owner's own requests and automations, for example answering questions about the owner's schedule, flagging conflicts, and creating or changing events. Data is never processed on behalf of anyone else.

Data Sharing and Selling

No data is sold, rented, or traded, and none is used for advertising. There are no advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or marketing partners involved.

Providing Huginn's features does require sending some data to services the owner has chosen. Data goes to these services only to carry out the owner's requests:

  • AI model providers. Huginn is powered by large language models. When a request involves calendar data, email or memory, the relevant content is sent to the model provider to generate a response. The current provider is Anthropic, through its API. The owner may configure others.
  • GitHub. Huginn syncs notes from its memory to a private repository owned by the owner. Those notes may include details derived from the data above.
  • Web search providers. Search queries Huginn makes for the owner go to a search provider, currently Tavily, and could reflect details of a request.
  • Tailscale. The owner's devices reach Huginn over a private Tailscale network.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens, cached calendar data, conversations and memory are stored on the owner's private homelab infrastructure, in a database run by the owner. Apart from the services listed under Data Sharing and Selling, there is no cloud database or managed service holding this data.

Security measures

  • Stored OAuth refresh tokens and client secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Temporary access-token files can be read only by the service that uses them.
  • Huginn is reached over the owner's private network and is not offered on a public address.
  • Secrets are not committed to source control.

Data Retention and Deletion

Tokens are retained only as long as they are useful for the owner's automations and can be revoked at any time through the issuing provider's account settings (for Google data, via Google Account permissions). Revoking access stops Huginn from retrieving new data, but does not by itself delete data already stored.

Cached data, conversations and memory remain until the owner deletes them. Database backups are kept on the owner's hardware on a rolling schedule of 14 daily, 8 weekly and 12 monthly copies, so deleted data can persist in backups for about a year. Data sent to the services listed above is also subject to those services' own retention terms.

Use of Google User Data

Huginn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to provide features the owner uses directly. It is transferred only to the services listed above, as needed to provide those features at the owner's direction, or as needed for security or to comply with the law. It is never used for advertising, and Huginn does not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models. People do not read it except as needed for security, debugging, or when the owner chooses to view it.

Children's Privacy

Huginn is a single-user personal tool and is not directed to children. Huginn does not knowingly collect data from children, though the owner's calendar or email could mention them.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL and the effective date at the top of the page will be revised. There is no mailing list or notification system; the published page is the single source of truth.
